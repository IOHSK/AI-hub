New to AI?

Built on the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers (AI CFT, 2024), start in three steps.

01 / Understand

Understand AI

Get a feel for what narrow and generative AI can and can't do, in a kinesiology context.

02 / Apply

Apply to teaching & research

Try it safely on real tasks like lesson design, feedback, and literature review.

03 / Reflect

Reflect critically

Check for bias, privacy, and academic integrity, and keep your use responsible.

Human-centered AI

Responsible, human-centered AI

What to weigh before reaching for a tool. People stay in charge of students, data, and judgment.

People decide

AI drafts and assists; educators and researchers stay responsible for evaluation and decisions.

Verify sources

Check the evidence behind generated content, and keep citations and sources transparent.

Protect privacy

Keep students' and participants' personal or sensitive data out of the tools.

Widen inclusion

Use it to improve accessibility and equity, and check that it doesn't widen gaps.

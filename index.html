New to AI?

Whatever your field in kinesiology, a simple three-step path to using AI with confidence. Tap a step to see more.

Generative AI creates text, images, or code from your instructions. Think of it as a fast, tireless assistant, not a source of truth. These are the terms worth knowing first.

Generative AITools that create text, images, or code from your prompts, like Claude or ChatGPT.
LLMA large language model, the engine behind most text assistants.
PromptThe instruction you give a tool. Clearer prompts give better results.
HallucinationWhen AI states something false as if it were true. Always verify.

Start with low-risk tasks where you can check the output yourself. Common wins across kinesiology:

  • Drafting and rewriting: outlines, summaries, feedback, emails
  • Explaining concepts and brainstorming at your level
  • Speeding up literature triage and organizing notes
  • Turning messy data into tables, charts, and a first-pass analysis
  • Preparing materials in multiple languages for a global audience
  • Drafting rubrics, protocols, and study instruments

AI supports your judgment; it does not replace it. Keep these checks in view every time.

  • It can sound confident yet be wrong, so verify facts and sources
  • It may invent citations or numbers, so check every reference
  • Never paste students or participants private data into a tool
  • Be transparent about where and how you used AI
  • Watch for bias, and check that it widens access rather than gaps
  • You stay responsible for every decision and result

Human-centered AI

Responsible, human-centered AI

What to weigh before reaching for a tool. People stay in charge of students, data, and judgment.

People decide

AI drafts and assists; educators and researchers stay responsible for evaluation and decisions.

Verify sources

Check the evidence behind generated content, and keep citations and sources transparent.

Protect privacy

Keep students' and participants' personal or sensitive data out of the tools.

Widen inclusion

Use it to improve accessibility and equity, and check that it doesn't widen gaps.
